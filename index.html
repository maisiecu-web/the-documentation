Dear Future Husband,

The story goes, I was bored, as most of my shenanigans start this way. Madame Bovary was bored and ruined her life for fun. Frankly inefficient, and a lack of imagination. Olivia Rodrigo was bored and, allegedly, stalked the poor man on the internet. Frankly common. I, however, do not succumb to the entertainment of social media, and only use it for the well-intended purpose of shitposting. So. I was bored and decided to make you a time capsule.

Like most things, I'm documenting it. My current life, my perpetual singleness, my avoidance of actually going on dates, my habit of making everything into a bit, my increasingly unhinged sense of humour (apparently everything I've committed to so far this year is a bit, this included), all of it, and hopefully one day these letters become yours.

And yeah, I really hope you like reading, because, well, your future wife writes a shit ton. So.

Sir, I have an extensive written record, a complicated personality, a penchant for antics, and a history of questionable adventures. Please familiarize yourself with the documentation.

Consider this your first letter.

M

P.S. Love is patient, love is kind (1 Corinthians 13:4). Please read that less as a verse and more as a job description, because your future wife is not for the weak.

P.P.S. Honestly, I don't know how you'll find your way to me. Even while starting this project, I'm actively avoiding dates, turning people down, and steadily becoming a public menace. So. No pressure. Take the scenic route if you have to.
